# Product Vision

## The game in one sentence

**Kingdom Legends** is a premium collectible-card game in which players discover the heroes, events and artifacts of the Bible — opening packs that feel like real foil, holding cards that catch the light, and walking through the stories behind them.

## Experience pillars

1. **Collectibles worth holding.** Every card is a physical object: thickness, a holographic finish that responds to touch and tilt, metal frames that signal rarity, and artwork composed as an illustration rather than a template. The pack is torn by hand, the cards are drawn out, and each one is revealed with weight and timing.
2. **Faithful to Scripture.** Verses are quoted word for word from a properly licensed translation (the public-domain Berean Standard Bible). Summaries and dramatizations are always labeled as such, never presented as Scripture. Cards describe moments of a story — never spiritual "power".
3. **Discovery through story.** Cards lead into the passages they come from. Journey chapters combine narrative, Scripture, questions, small hands-on discoveries and a personal reflection. Learning is never locked behind getting an answer right.
4. **A fair, calm economy.** Discovery Packs are free. Randomized items are never sold, nothing for sale affects play, and nothing is lost by taking a break. Rewards are granted by the server, exactly once.
5. **Ancient royal luxury.** Obsidian, stone and parchment; gold reserved for what matters; restrained motion that becomes cinematic only at the moments that deserve it.
6. **For everyone.** Every gesture has a button, every celebration can be skipped, reduced-motion is honored, rarity is shown by shape and label (not color alone), and text scales with the system.

## Core loop

```
Royal Hall ──▶ Royal Treasury (daily pack) ──▶ tear · draw · reveal ──▶ Royal Archive
     ▲                                                                       │
     └──── Journey chapters (story, Scripture, rewards) ◀── Artifact Chamber ┘
```

- **Royal Hall** (home): the featured legend, today's pack, the current chapter and the archive at a glance.
- **Royal Treasury**: the pack-opening ritual — the centerpiece of the vertical slice.
- **Royal Archive**: the binder of every card, discovered or not.
- **Artifact Chamber**: one card, large, to be turned in the light and read.
- **Journey**: an atlas of campaigns and their chapters.
- **Challenges**: short Scripture trivia rounds.
- **Shop**: cosmetics only, in a later release.

## What success looks like for Milestone 1

A player can, on a phone, in a few minutes: enter the kingdom, claim a free pack, tear it open with a finger, draw the cards out, reveal five cards culminating in a cinematic Legendary, inspect that card and read its verse, and play the David chapter that the Legendary leads into — with everything honest about being a prototype.

## Out of scope (for now)

Trading, battles or any competitive mode; accounts and cloud sync (Milestone 2); paid products of any kind; user-generated content; social features. See [IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md](IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md).
