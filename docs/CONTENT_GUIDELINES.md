# Content Guidelines

These rules apply to every card, quest, trivia question, piece of copy and artwork.

## Scripture

- **Translation:** the Berean Standard Bible (BSB), dedicated to the public domain by BSB Publishing, LLC. The attribution appears wherever Scripture is quoted at length (quests, Settings → About).
- **Verbatim only.** Verse text comes from `src/content/scripture/bsb.generated.ts`, generated by `scripts/extract-scripture.mjs` from the published BSB text. Never retype, abridge or "modernize" a verse.
- **Excerpts are marked.** When an excerpt begins or ends mid-sentence or mid-quotation, the UI adds an ellipsis (`passageText`). The words of the verse are never changed.
- **References** are displayed with `formatReference` ("1 Samuel 17:45", "Psalm 23:1").
- Adding a verse: add it to `VERSES` in the extraction script, regenerate, and reference it from content; `content.test.ts` fails if any reference is missing.

## Three kinds of text, always distinguishable

| Kind | Example | How it is shown |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Scripture** | 1 Samuel 17:47 | Scripture typeface, quotation marks, reference + "BSB" |
| **Summary** | Card summaries | Labeled "Summary"; must be faithful to the passage |
| **Dramatization** | Quest narrative steps | Labeled "Dramatization": "An artistic retelling to set the scene." |

## Cards

- A card depicts **one moment of a story** ("David — The Giant Slayer"), never a power level. Themes are character qualities shown in the story (Courage, Trust), not statistics.
- No spiritual ratings, holiness scores or "power" numbers — on cards, levels or anywhere else.
- **Card ids are permanent** once published; never rename or reuse them (inventories reference them).
- Every card carries `editorial` metadata: `status` (`draft` → `editorial_review` → `art_review` → `qa` → `published`), a `revision` number, and `placeholderArt`.
- All Milestone 1 card text is **DRAFT** awaiting biblical editorial review; all art is a placeholder (see below).

## Quests

- Each quest is anchored in a passage and alternates story, Scripture, questions, a hands-on discovery and a reflection.
- **Learning is never locked behind being right**: any answer advances, and every answer is followed by the explanation and the verse.
- Wrong answers are met gently ("Not quite — here is what the passage says."), never with failure sounds.
- Reflections are optional and private: Milestone 1 does not store what the player writes.

## Trivia

- Every question must be answered by a verse quoted in the app; the verse is shown after answering (`src/content/trivia.ts`).
- Distractors must be plausible but clearly wrong according to the passage, and must not be other biblical events that could confuse younger players.

## Artwork

- **Original only.** No copied or traced artwork from other games or publishers (in particular, nothing derived from Pokémon or similar card games), no unlicensed stock, fonts, music or translations.
- Milestone 1 art is procedural (`src/graphics/art`): figures are silhouettes, rim-lit against the light of the scene. This keeps depictions respectful and avoids inventing faces for biblical people.
- **God is never depicted as a figure.** Divine action is shown through light, fire, water or wind (Elijah's fire, the divided sea, the rainbow).
- Scenes follow the text: the shield-bearer walks before Goliath (1 Samuel 17:7), David takes five smooth stones from the brook (17:40), the walls of water stand on the right and the left (Exodus 14:22), Ruth and Naomi travel from Moab toward Bethlehem.
- Every scene must read as a silhouette, because undiscovered cards show only the silhouette.
- Placeholder art is labeled as such in the Artifact Chamber until commissioned illustrations replace it.

## Copy

- Calm, dignified, warm. No casino language ("jackpot", "big win"), no countdown pressure, no guilt.
- Be honest about the prototype: demo packs say "fixed contents", the shop says nothing can be bought, practice trivia says scores are not saved.

## Review workflow (planned "Kingdom Legends Studio")

Draft → Biblical editorial review → Artwork review → Quality assurance → Published. Every edit bumps `revision`; published ids are frozen.
